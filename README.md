# Bright-Learn-Coffee-Shop-
Coffee shop Case Study
COFFEE SHOP ANALYSIS

Overview This project delivers a 13‑slide PowerPoint presentation for the coffee shop. The presentation is designed to showcase the store’s background, performance metrics,  and future strategy. It includes multiple graphs, slicers, maps, and dashboards to make the content interactive and visually appealing. The goal is to provide a comprehensive business overview with actionable recommendations.

Process Data Preparation: Import and clean the dataset (sales, products, locations, KPIs).

Visualization: Generate graphs (bar, line, pie charts) for sales, customer footfall, KPIs, and product performance.

Interactivity: Add slicers for filtering by product, location, and time period.

Design: Apply a clean, professional template with a subtle background to ensure readability.

Content Structuring: Organize slides into logical sections (background, performance, insights, recommendations, closing).

Finalization: Review layout, ensure clarity, and align visuals with business insights.

Summary The presentation highlights:
Coffee shop background and mission

Store locations (map with 3 branches)

Top 10 best‑selling items and unique product

Monthly revenue trends (with highest revenue month identified)

KPI dashboard (revenue, transaction value, retention rate)

Customer satisfaction and marketing performance

Insights and recommendations for growth

Future strategy and closing message

This structure ensures stakeholders gain a clear understanding of performance, opportunities, and next steps.

Tools Used Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, preparation, and chart creation
PowerPoint: Slide design, layout, and integration of visuals

Slicers (Excel/Power BI): Interactive filtering for graphs

Mapping Tool (PowerPoint/Excel): Visualizing store locations

Design Template: Professional background ensuring readability
